librotenado/0.1 · draft

An open protocol for accounting data in Europe

Every European country has its own e-invoices, audit files, bank statements and charts of accounts. librotenado is one harmonised model for all of it – bookings, receipts, invoices, reports and filings – with the existing formats as exact translations. Open for every vendor, free to implement.

One model, many translations

Standards exist for the parts: EN 16931 describes an invoice, camt.053 a bank statement, SAF-T an audit extract, XBRL a financial statement. None of them describes the books as a whole. librotenado does – and treats every existing format as a translation of its model: unambiguous, reversible where the format allows, versioned and tested per country.

Principles

The model is the reference

Formats, countries and products are described in relation to one model. Where a country distinguishes more finely, the model grows – for everyone.

The original stays the receipt

An incoming e-invoice or bank statement is kept byte for byte. Translations make it usable; they never replace it.

Nothing is overwritten

Books are append-only events. A correction is a reversal that refers to what it corrects.

Money is exact

Amounts are exact counts of a currency’s smallest unit, never floating point. Every booking balances for every owner in every currency.

Conflicts are visible

When concurrent work breaks a rule of accounting, a person decides. Nothing is merged silently.

Private by design

Books are end-to-end encrypted between the people and devices that share them. Whoever relays them cannot read them.

Built for synchronisation

A set of books is a growing set of immutable events. Each device chains its own events by hash and stamps them with a hybrid logical clock, so every device agrees on one order. Two copies merge by union – in any order, any number of times, with the same result. A fork, a gap or a broken link in a chain is reported, and so is a booking reversed twice: the protocol puts it on a review list instead of guessing.

Events

Immutable, identified by their SHA-256 hash, chained per device.

Hybrid logical clocks

An order that respects causality and stays close to real time.

Relays and backends

Relays store and forward encrypted events; a backend is a trusted peer that can do more.

Status: version 0.4

The specification comes with a reference library in Rust that implements it, and with German law as the first country: e-invoices, bank statements, the statutory reports and the tax auditor’s data transfer. Everything is a draft – open for discussion and changes before 1.0; the German mappings wait for review by tax advisers.
